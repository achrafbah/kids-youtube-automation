# Privacy Policy for Tales of Wonder Automation

Last updated: October 2026

Tales of Wonder Automation ("we", "our", or "the application") is an automated educational content management tool designed to publish educational kids stories and animations to social media platforms including YouTube and TikTok.

## Information We Access
- We only access video upload and publishing permissions necessary to schedule and post videos created by the content owner.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data.
- User data is processed strictly for authentication and publishing purposes.
